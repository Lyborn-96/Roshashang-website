Our Services

Recording, transcription and translation.

Everything from recording a three-day inquiry to transcribing a single interview, delivered in the format your matter requires.

Recording

Recording of hearings and meetings

We record professional conduct inquiries, disciplinary hearings, arbitrations and meetings, either on virtual platforms such as Microsoft Teams or in person at your venue. Recording is quoted per hearing, and the transcript follows from the same recording.

  • Virtual hearings on Microsoft Teams and Zoom
  • In-person recording at your venue
  • Multi-day matters recorded and transcribed in volumes
Quoted per hearingVirtual or in person
Legal

Legal & disciplinary transcription

Transcripts of professional conduct inquiries, preliminary inquiries, disciplinary and grievance hearings, CCMA and bargaining council arbitrations and court records, prepared in volumes with a cover page listing the panel and parties.

  • Cover page with chairperson, members and representatives
  • Page header with matter, page number and date
  • Line numbers every ten lines and underlined speaker labels
  • Delivered as e-transcripts in PDF and Word
E-transcriptsVolumes
Specialist

Medical, legal and technical transcription

Matters with clinical, legal or engineering terminology are handled by experienced transcribers and checked by a reviewer for names, terms and figures. Specialist transcription is delivered within three working days.

Recordings in Xitsonga, isiZulu and Setswana are transcribed and translated into English. Where a witness switches language during a hearing, the translation appears in the transcript where it was spoken.

Our Process

How every matter is handled.

Each matter gets one reference number that follows it from booking to invoice.

01

Book or upload

Book us to record, or send us the recording securely. An NDA covers the matter from the start.

02

Transcribe

The matter is assigned by language and turnaround, and transcribed to your template.

03

Review

A second transcriber checks the transcript against the audio before release.

04

Deliver & invoice

E-transcripts are delivered in PDF and Word, and the invoice follows on your account terms.
