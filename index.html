About Roshashang

A South African transcription company built on accuracy and discretion.

Founded by Alfred Mkhabele and registered in 2013, Roshashang (Pty) Ltd serves regulators, universities, state entities, law firms and private clients from Kempton Park, with work across Gauteng, Limpopo and Mpumalanga.

Our full-time transcribers and trainees work to each client's template, and every transcript is checked before it is delivered. Training young South Africans as professional transcribers is part of how we work.

Why Roshashang

Built for confidential, high-stakes records.

Confidential by default

Recordings and transcripts are handled under a non-disclosure agreement, and only the assigned team can open them.

Your format, every time

Cover pages, volumes, numbered lines, speaker labels and certification, exactly as your matter requires.

Clear, published rates

From R25.00 per audio minute, with urgent and overnight turnaround priced upfront.

Four languages

English, Xitsonga, isiZulu and Setswana, with translation into English.

Recording and transcription in one

We can record the hearing ourselves, so the transcript starts from a clean recording.

Accounts that suit you

Billed per matter, case number or order number. SLA clients pay on 30-day terms.
