FAQ

Questions clients ask us.

Working with us

How do I send you a recording?

Attach a recording of up to 10 MB to the quote form, or ask us for a secure upload link for larger files. Please don't send confidential recordings by WhatsApp or email.

Can you record the hearing for us?

Yes. We record virtual hearings on platforms such as Microsoft Teams and attend in person at your venue. Recording is charged per day.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Xitsonga, isiZulu and Setswana. Recordings in African languages can be transcribed in the original language, translated into English, or both.

Do you work outside Gauteng?

Yes. We work across Gauteng, Limpopo and Mpumalanga, and record virtual hearings for clients anywhere in South Africa.

Pricing & payment

How much does transcription cost?

R25.00 per audio minute for normal turnaround (7 working days), R45.00 for urgent specialist transcription (3 working days) and R55.30 for extreme urgent or overnight work. See the rates page for examples.

Do you charge VAT?

No. Roshashang is not registered as a VAT vendor, so our rates exclude VAT and no VAT is added.

When do I pay?

Clients on a Service Level Agreement pay within 30 days of invoice. Once-off clients pay upfront.

Can you invoice per case or order number?

Yes. We invoice per matter, case number, order number or volume, whichever your finance team needs.

Formats & delivery

What format will the transcript be in?

E-transcripts in PDF and Word, in your template: a cover page with the panel and parties, a header with the matter, page number and date, numbered lines and underlined speaker labels.

How fast can you deliver?

Normal turnaround is 7 working days, urgent is 3 working days, and extreme urgent work can be delivered overnight or the same day.

Can I track progress?

Yes. Every matter gets a reference number, and we confirm the expected delivery date and keep you updated until the transcript is delivered.

Confidentiality

Is my recording kept confidential?

Yes. Matters are covered by a non-disclosure agreement, only the assigned transcriber and reviewer can open the recording, and every access is logged.

How do you handle personal information?

We process personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Read our privacy notice.

Get started

Need a recording transcribed?

Send us the recording and your deadline. We'll confirm the rate and delivery date before we start.
