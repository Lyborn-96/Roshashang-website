Privacy notice.

How Roshashang collects, uses and protects personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013.

Responsible party: Roshashang (Pty) Ltd, Reg. No. 2013/106429/07, 07 Queen Ann Str, Terenure, Kempton Park, 1619. Contact: info@roshashang.co.za.

What we collect

  • Contact details of clients and their representatives
  • Recordings and the personal information spoken in them
  • Transcripts we prepare, and billing records

Why we use it

Only to record, transcribe, translate and deliver your matter, to invoice for that work, and to meet our legal obligations.

How we protect it

  • Every matter is covered by a non-disclosure agreement
  • Only the assigned transcriber and reviewer can open a recording
  • Access to recordings and transcripts is logged
  • Recordings and transcripts are sent and received through secure channels

How long we keep it

Recordings and transcripts are kept for as long as your Service Level Agreement specifies, then deleted or returned to you.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to its processing, by writing to info@roshashang.co.za. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.
