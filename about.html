About Us

Accurate, confidential and proudly South African.

Roshashang (Pty) Ltd has recorded and transcribed the proceedings of regulators, universities, state entities and law firms since 2013.

Who we are

The people behind the record.

Roshashang was founded by Alfred Mkhabele to give South African institutions a dependable partner for recording and transcribing the proceedings that matter: professional conduct inquiries, disciplinary hearings, arbitrations, council meetings and research interviews.

Our team of full-time transcribers is supported by trainees who learn the craft on live matters under supervision. Every transcript is reviewed before delivery, and trainee work is always checked by a senior transcriber.

We recognise the challenge of unemployment in our country. Training and employing young transcribers is how we answer it, and we support the government's broad-based black economic empowerment and employment equity policies.

Mission

A strategic partner to the organisations we serve.

  • Give our clients a competitive advantage through accurate records
  • Provide services that improve the workplace
  • Promote quality and efficient practice in our industry
  • Work with all stakeholders to achieve common goals
Vision

Professional records that create jobs.

To provide professional, strategic, efficient and cost-effective end-to-end transcription solutions that are instrumental to job creation in our communities and across South Africa.

Where we work

Based in Kempton Park, working across three provinces.

We serve clients in Gauteng, Limpopo and Mpumalanga, including Pretoria, Polokwane, Tzaneen and Ermelo. Virtual hearings are recorded and transcribed for clients anywhere in South Africa.
